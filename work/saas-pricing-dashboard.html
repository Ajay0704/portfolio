      Method

      198 well-known B2B SaaS companies (18 in each of 11 categories) were read from their public pricing pages in August 2026; 188 had usable pricing. An October 2026 audit re-checked disputed rows against archived copies of the August pages; a blinded recheck then found the AI field under-counted, so every AI row coded as bundled or unclear was re-coded from the archived pages. 137 field corrections are logged with sources. This describes leading SaaS, not all SaaS, and measures visible list pricing only: AI or SSO sold through enterprise contracts never appears on a pricing page.

      Definitions

      “Charges extra for AI” = AI only on pricier plans, sold as an add-on, or metered by credits with a way to buy more. “SSO tax” = SSO only on the top paid plan or an Enterprise contract. Ranges are 95% Wilson intervals.
